# DinDrift — Competitor Ad Intelligence (Meta Ad Library)

_Source: Meta Ad Library, ACTIVE ads, pulled 2026-06-02 via Facebook Ads MCP._

## Market snapshot
- **Denmark:** Thin. Only one direct competitor actively advertising — **"AI Automatisering"** (page 1204747729378257), running 3 near-identical "AI for danske SMV'er" ads. Risk: Meta Andromeda clustering (>60% similarity) suppresses near-duplicate creative. **Opportunity: Danish-language AI-agent advertising is essentially uncontested.**
- **Secondary DK:** "Inthemaking" — grant angle ("Få op til 150.000 kr. i tilskud").
- **English market (US/GB):** 86+ active ads. Mature angle playbook below.

## Top 5 winning angles (ranked)

1. **Hours-back / time freedom** — "Save 10–30 hours every week with AI." (Vistara: AI for Agencies). Quantified, believable time savings.
2. **AI employee, not a chatbot** — "Not a Chatbot. A Coworker." (Junior.so). Reframes commodity chatbot as a hire; higher perceived value.
3. **Loss / churn prevention** — "Losing 15–30% of your clients every month? Here's the service that stops it." (Lead Oracle AI). Loss aversion + specific stat.
4. **Done-for-you / no-code, fast** — "Build & launch your own AI agent in a few hours, even if you've never written a line of code." (Kunaal Naik). Kills the too-technical/too-slow objection.
5. **Niche + transparent price** — "AI for realtors — from $49/mo." (Rapidline AI; Scale Certified vertical packs). Vertical specificity + price anchor.

## Gap DinDrift should own
Danish-language, founder-led, anti-agency ("ingen account managers, intet bureau-overhead"). No DK competitor combines proven English angles with local language + no-agency trust.
